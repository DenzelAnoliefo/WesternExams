WesternExams — Past Exams & Midterms for Western University Courses

WesternExams is a free, student-built archive of past exams, midterms, and final exams for courses at Western University (UWO) in London, Ontario. Search the archive by course code — for example CS1027, CALC1000, or BIOL1001 — preview exam PDFs in your browser, and contribute past papers to help other students prepare.

What you can do on WesternExams

  • Exam search — find past exams by course code, professor, or year.
  • PDF preview — read exams directly in your browser before downloading.
  • Contribute — upload past exams to help fellow Western students.
  • Always free — no subscriptions and no paywalls, for every Western course.

The archive spans hundreds of Western University courses across all faculties, including Science, Engineering, Social Science, Health Sciences, and the Ivey Business School.
